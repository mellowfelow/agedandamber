## Phase 2 — Category Keyword Table & Structural Observation

### Structural observation (flagging only, not restructuring)
**"Craft Cocktails" itself is a weak hub anchor.** As shown above, the bare phrase and its close variants pull almost entirely bar/restaurant-navigational search intent in the real data (`craft cocktails near me` 3,600, `craft cocktail bar` 1,600, dozens of specific venue names) — not people looking to buy a bottle or can online. Meanwhile this subcategory's 44 real SKUs split into four genuinely different product types with different buyer language: spirit-based RTD cocktails (Cutwater, On The Rocks, Tip Top, etc. — buyers search "canned cocktails," "ready to drink cocktails," "premixed cocktails"), malt-based hard seltzers (White Claw, Truly, Topo Chico, Nütrl — buyers search "hard seltzer," a term that carries huge, well-established national volume but doesn't appear at all in this data pull), hard iced teas/lemonades (Twisted Tea, Surfside, Lipton, AriZona, Mike's, Simply Spiked, Smirnoff Ice — buyers search "hard iced tea" / "spiked lemonade," also absent from this pull), and canned wine (Underwood, Babe, Bev, House Wine — buyers search "canned wine"). Recommend, without making the call: (1) don't use "craft cocktails" as the on-page H1/title anchor — use "canned cocktails" or "ready-to-drink cocktails" instead, which are real, CSV-backed, and lower-difficulty; (2) consider whether finer-grained hub pages (a hard-seltzer page, a hard-iced-tea page) would serve real search behavior better than one blended hub, given zero query overlap was found between the "cocktail" terms and the seltzer/tea product types in this data.
