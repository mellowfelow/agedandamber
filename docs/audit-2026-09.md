## CRITICAL

### C1 — 180 of 268 blog posts are future-dated (up to 2026-12-09; today is 2026-09-09)
`Article` schema `datePublished`/`dateModified` and sitemap `<lastmod>` all emit future ISO dates. Google treats future-dated content as a quality/trust problem and may suppress it; "freshness" signals are inverted. **Fix:** clamp every `isoDate` to ≤ today, stagger historically, and stop letting the sitemap emit future `lastmod`.

### C2 — `SearchAction` schema points at a dead capability
Homepage `WebSite.potentialAction` → `https://www.agedandamber.com/shop/?q={search_term_string}`, and `llms.txt` advertises `/api/search/?q=`. **`/shop/` does not read the `q` param** (verified live — `/shop/?q=macallan` returns the unfiltered page). WebForge Rule 10 / crosscheck B2 ship-stopper: declared capability must be real. **Fix:** make `ShopView` read `?searchParams.q` on load (and ideally render a server-filtered first page), or drop the `SearchAction`.

### C3 — ~493 brand-hub pages are thin / templated at scale
1 brand hub has **0** products ("coming soon", still in sitemap); **351 have only 1–2** products. All 493 use formula-generated title/H1/meta/CTA from `brandSeo()`. ~70% of the brand-hub layer is thin, near-duplicate, indexable content — classic doorway-page / index-bloat risk for the whole domain. **Fix options:** `noindex` (keep crawlable/linked) any hub with < 3 products; merge thin brands into their subcategory hub; or gate hub generation on a real product-count threshold. Drop empty hubs from the sitemap regardless.

### C4 — 224 toxic backlinks from ~118 domains, built in one week
Semrush: 224 backlinks / 118 referring domains, up +1,936% / +973%, ~92 new referring domains in the single week 24–31 Aug. "Follow backlinks not found." Uniformly toxic: hacked gov/edu pages (`tandem.edu.co`, `arsiv.csgb.gov.ct.tr`) with injected commercial anchors ("hennessy vsop", "best whiskey"), foreign forum farms (`rufox.com`, `diskutuje.cz`, `newsvuse.com`), a literal `test - Internal` link. Bing WMT independently flags low-quality links. Pattern is consistent with negative SEO. **Fix:** export the full referring-domain list from Semrush + GSC Links, build `disavow.txt` covering every domain (none is worth keeping), submit in GSC, and monitor weekly.

### C5 — Contact / inquiry form reports success even when the send fails
`ContactView` does `fetch('/api/inquiry/').catch(()=>{})` then unconditionally shows "Message Dispatched". If Zoho SMTP creds are wrong or the route 500s, every inquiry is silently lost while the user is told it worked. Same fire-and-forget pattern likely in the wholesale form. WebForge forms non-negotiable. **Fix:** await the response, show an on-page error + email/phone fallback on failure, only show success on 200. (`CLAUDE.md` still says forms use Web3Forms — actual is Zoho SMTP via `notify.ts`; fix the doc too.)

## HIGH

### H1 — Every product title & meta is auto-generated; 630/1,347 titles overflow the SERP
No product has a custom `seo.titleTag` or `seo.metaDescription` (0/1,347). Titles fall back to `"<name> — Buy Online" | Aged And Amber`; with the template appended, **630 exceed ~60 chars** (e.g. "Buffalo Trace Kentucky Straight Bourbon — Buy Online | Aged And Amber" = 66). **300 meta descriptions are < 70 chars** (the one-line `shortDescription`). At 1,347 pages this is the biggest on-page opportunity. **Fix:** generate templated-but-varied titles that fit (brand + type + "Buy Online" without the site-name tail on long ones), and expand thin meta descriptions.

### H2 — Product / hub pages have no `priority` on the LCP image
`ProductDetailView` and hub grids use `SmartImage`/`next/image` with no `priority`, so the above-the-fold product image lazy-loads. With `images.unoptimized: true` there's also no `srcset` — every grid thumbnail downloads the full-res file. Affects LCP on all ~1,347 product pages + every category/hub grid. **Fix:** `priority` on the main product image and first-row grid images; consider a small pre-resized thumbnail variant.

### H3 — Brand-name inconsistency sitewide
`SITE.name` = **"Aged And Amber"** (schema, llms.txt, OG `site_name`, title template) but page titles/metadata/hero say **"Aged & Amber"**. Some titles read "About Aged & Amber … | Aged And Amber". WebForge AI-visibility: the brand entity must be spelled identically everywhere. Pick one ("Aged & Amber" reads best) and normalise `SITE.name`, the title template, schema, and all `.well-known` descriptions (which are also inconsistent: "Boutique Craft Whiskey & Spirits" vs "Napa Valley's Allocated Spirits, Fine Wine & Rare Beverage Cellar").

### H4 — 258/268 blog posts share ~21 recycled category stock images
Only 45 distinct `image` values across 268 posts; 258 use a generic `/images/categories/*.webp` tile (14+ posts literally share one scotch photo). That image is the article hero, the OG image, and the `Article` schema `image`. Weak for E-E-A-T, social, and AI citation at scale. **Fix:** article-specific images (even 1 per topic cluster is a big step up).

### H5 — Missing legal / policy pages
No `/privacy`, `/terms`, `/shipping`, or `/refund` routes exist. Footer links to none of them. The cookie banner names no privacy policy. For an alcohol DTC store this hurts trust signals, Google Merchant eligibility, and GDPR/CCPA posture. Return/shipping policy content exists only inside `JsonLd`/`llms.txt`, not as real pages. **Fix:** add the four policy pages, link them in the footer, point the cookie banner at `/privacy`.

### H6 — Google Merchant Center not connected; only 8 valid product listings
GSC surfaces "Display your 433 products on the Shopping tab" — Merchant Center is not linked. Merchant listings report shows **8 valid items** of 1,347, with recurring "missing `handlingTime`" in `offers.shippingDetails.deliveryTime` and intermittent missing `hasMerchantReturnPolicy`/`shippingDetails` (partly stale crawls). `Product.brand` is also set to the retailer, not the real producer. **Fix:** add `handlingTime` to the shipping schema; set `Product.brand` to the actual producer (e.g. "Buffalo Trace"); connect a Merchant Center feed once indexing recovers (post-C3).

### H7 — No Content-Security-Policy header
Verified absent on `/` and product pages. WebForge wants a CSP whitelisting the form endpoint, Tawk.to, and `wss:`. **Fix:** add `Content-Security-Policy` to `vercel.json` headers (report-only first, then enforce).

## MEDIUM

- **M1 — Thin footer internal linking.** Footer surfaces only 3 of 21 categories; the mega-menu (all category links) is client-gated (`shopDropdownOpen && …`) so it's absent from server HTML. Category/hub link equity is weak. Add a full category list + top brand hubs to the footer (server-rendered).
- **M2 — `docs/PROJECT.md` is badly stale.** Describes a 3-category bourbon/scotch/tequila shop with $110–$480 pricing. Reality: 21 categories, 1,347 products, **median price $27.99**, 955 items under $50, min-order $200. The whole "allocated / rare cask / direct allocation" framing on Corona, Jim Beam ($30), etc. is not credible and risks reading as misleading. Decide positioning, then align PROJECT.md, CLAUDE.md, copy, and schema.
- **M3 — `CLAUDE.md` is stale/wrong.** Says "Form submission uses Web3Forms CORS integration" — actual is Zoho SMTP via `notify.ts`. Says catalog is "bourbon, single malt scotch, and rare tequila". Update.
- **M4 — Product-page copy is whiskey-templated for the whole catalog.** "USD per 750ml bottle" on 12 oz beer cans; "Organoleptic Tasting Profile" / "Cask Wood Finish" / "{proof} Proof ({proof/2}% ABV)" on beer, wine, vodka, NA. Factually wrong on ~350 products. Template needs per-category variants.
- **M5 — `create_order_draft` MCP tool is a stub.** Ignores `items`, returns the generic `/shop/` URL as `checkoutUrl` with `status: "draft_created"`. Either build real draft pre-fill or make the response honestly say "add these to cart: <links>".
- **M6 — Contact form (and likely wholesale) inputs have no labels** — placeholder-only. WCAG 2.2 AA fail. Add visually-hidden `<label>`s.
- **M7 — GDPR banner is homepage-only** (`if (pathname !== '/') return`). A visitor landing on a product page from Google never sees it. Show on first visit regardless of entry page; add a real reject option or drop the "Accept All" framing.
- **M8 — About page has no `AboutPage`/`Organization` JSON-LD** and is ~450 words (WebForge target ~700+). Add schema and expand the entity narrative.
- **M9 — NAP inconsistency.** Phone `+1 (562) …` is a Long Beach area code; HQ is stated as "Napa Valley & San Francisco". Address `72 Bellevue Ave, Napa` — confirm it's real before it goes in `LocalBusiness`-style schema.
- **M10 — Sitemap is a single flat file** (2,155 URLs, no split, no `<image:image>`). Fine for size, but WebForge v9 wants a sitemap index + image sitemap for the product URLs. Also every `lastmod` is "now" on every build (churns the whole sitemap daily).
- **M11 — Duplicate products / copy.** ~27 products share an identical `shortDescription` and ~29 share an identical `fullDescription` (Corona Extra, Allagash White, Bell's Two Hearted, etc. appear twice — once per subcategory). Near-duplicate product pages. De-dupe or differentiate.
- **M12 — Slow TTFB on a cluster of pages.** champagne-sparkling product pages + several blog posts took 5–11 s on first hit during the crawl (cold render). Re-check in Lighthouse; if they're not in the prerender manifest, force static.
- **M13 — hardcoded "Last updated: August 2026"** string on hub + category pages. Will age badly; drive from data or remove.

- L2 — `.well-known/ucp` and `.well-known/openid-configuration` serve as `application/octet-stream` (documented Vercel platform limitation for extensionless `.well-known` files — headers/middleware bypass them). Accept or move to `.json` + a redirect.

- L4 — `auth.md` "Public Resources" table omits api-catalog / agent-skills / server-card.

- L7 — `not-found.tsx` sets no `robots: noindex` metadata (Next serves 404 status, so low impact).
- L8 — `crosscheck.mjs` is a 7-line stub checking `public/llms.txt` (which doesn't exist — it's a route); `package.json` has no `crosscheck`/`images` script. The WebForge pre-ship gate isn't actually wired up.
