## Phase 1 — Filter Summary

This was the noisiest export of the whole project — vodka's name collides with an enormous amount of unrelated content.

**Dropped categories:**
- **"Vodka sauce" / pasta recipes** — massive volume (`vodka sauce` 60,500, `penne alla vodka` 40,500, `vodka sauce recipe` 40,500, `rigatoni alla vodka` 14,800, and dozens more) — this is Italian food content, not the spirit. Fully excluded, ~15 rows totaling 250,000+ combined volume.
- **"Deep Eddy Pool"** — Austin's actual public swimming pool, psychotherapy practice, cabaret venue, carpet cleaners, all sharing the Deep Eddy name — ~25 rows dropped (`deep eddy pool`, `deep eddy municipal pool`, `deep eddy psychotherapy`, `deep eddy carpet`, etc.)
- **"Broken Shed" literal sheds** — roofing/carpentry/garden-shed noise (`broken shed door`, `broken garden shed`, `broke down shed`, etc.) — over 40 rows dropped, this contaminates the Broken Shed vodka brand's export more than any other brand.
- **Adult content** — "Addison Vodka" appears to be an adult performer's name, not a product (`addison vodka porn`, `addison vodka nude`, `addison vodka anal`) — dropped entirely, flagging in case this surfaces again in future exports.
- **Anime/gaming "Vodka"** — a horse-girl character named Vodka from the *Uma Musume* gacha game (`vodka uma musume`, `did daiwa scarlet and vodka grow up together`, `vodka horse`) — dropped, unrelated to the spirit.
- **People named Smirnoff** — comedian Yakov Smirnoff, dancer Karina Smirnoff — dropped.
- **Grey Goose non-spirit collisions** — a golf shoe brand ("grey goose shoes"), a Maryland wedding venue ("grey goose farm"), a Connecticut restaurant ("grey goose hampton va" / "grey goose southport") — dropped, ~15 rows.
- **Generic "haku" Japanese-language queries** unrelated to the vodka ("what does haku mean in japanese") — dropped.
- **NYC "Russian Vodka Room"** restaurant — dropped, not a product.
- **Smirnoff Ice** (`smirnoff ice`, 60,500 vol!) — this is real, but it's a flavored malt beverage line, not vodka. Not stocked, and arguably belongs to an RTD/Liqueurs category if you ever want it — flagged separately below, not folded into vodka category keywords.

**Kept but flagged as generic/category-level, not brand-specific:** the huge cluster of "how many calories in a shot of vodka," "is vodka gluten free," "does vodka expire/go bad," "what proof is vodka" style questions that repeat near-identically across nearly every brand — real, high-volume, and genuinely useful as **category-page FAQ content and blog fodder**, not something to duplicate on every single brand hub.
